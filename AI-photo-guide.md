# Praga Outlet: AI photo guide (ChatGPT Plus)

## 0. Setup (once)
1. In ChatGPT make a **Project** (name it "Praga photos").
2. Paste **Section A** into the Project's Instructions box.
3. Use a **normal chat inside the Project** (NOT the "Work" / agent mode) on the **Instant** model. The Work mode plans in steps and takes 5 to 10 minutes per image. A normal Instant chat is very fast. Do not use "thinking" unless one product keeps coming out wrong.
4. The man in the photos must have agreed (in writing) to have his photos used in the shop's ads and website.

## 1. For each product (follow in order)
1. Open a **new chat inside the Project**.
2. **Image 1, model full-body:** attach the product photo + the man's photo, send **Prompt B** with one pose line.
3. **Image 2, model close-up:** same chat. Attach the **product photo + the man's photo again**, send **Prompt D** with one framing line. Do NOT attach the first AI picture.
4. **Images 3 and 4, ghost mannequin front, then back:** send **Prompt C1** with the front photo. When it is done, in the same chat send **Prompt C2** with the back photo. Two short requests, one image each.
5. Check every image (Section 5), then upload to Shopify.
6. Next product: new chat.

Per product = 4 AI images, each one a short, separate request. Limit is about 40-50 images per 3 hours (unofficial, check the banner in the app), so about 10 products per 3 hours. Work in batches. Make 1 image per request, and only regenerate when something is wrong.

Exception: if the close-up looks like a different man or different outfit, add the first AI picture and write: "Use the first attached picture only for the man's face and outfit. Copy the product from the product photo." Then check the logo carefully.

## 2. SECTION A: Project instructions (paste once)

```
You create e-commerce photos for Praga Outlet, a men's branded clothing store in Lebanon.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- Use the uploaded product photo(s) as the only truth. Copy the product exactly: color, fabric look, logo, patches, buttons, zips, pockets, stripes, collar, cuffs, hem and fit.
- Never add, remove, move or redraw any logo, text or detail. If unsure about a detail, keep it as in the photo.
- Keep the real color of the product. Do not make it brighter or change its shade.
- Size: square, 1200x1200 pixels.
- FRAMING: wide shot, camera pulled back. The garment must be SMALLER in the picture, with a clear empty grey margin all around it: at least 12% of the picture width of empty space on the left and right, and at least 12% of the picture height on the top and bottom. The garment takes about three quarters of the picture width. It must never touch or come close to the edges. Centered.
- Background: plain flat light grey, color #DCDCDC, no texture, no props, no text.
- Soft, even studio light. Sharp, natural, realistic photo, like a real camera photo. Natural skin, no smoothing, no plastic or CGI look.
- No watermark, no captions, no extra people, no brand names added.
- Make only ONE image per request, unless I ask for more.

MODEL PHOTOS
- Use the EXACT man in the uploaded person photo. Keep his real face, hair, beard, skin and body. Do not change or beautify him.
- Plain dark trousers and plain shoes unless the product is trousers. Nothing with logos.
- The whole product must stay clearly visible. Natural, relaxed, confident poses, not stiff.
- I will tell you the pose for each image.

GHOST MANNEQUIN PHOTOS (professional invisible mannequin, like an official brand website photo)
- The garment looks worn by an invisible body, floating, perfectly front-facing and symmetrical.
- NO visible mannequin of any kind: no plastic or fabric mannequin, no neck block, no stand, no pole, no base, no hanger, no hands, no head, no body. Nothing but the garment.
- SLEEVES: both sleeves hang straight down along the sides of the body, slightly away from it (about 10 to 15 degrees), parallel and symmetrical. Same length, same angle, cuffs at the same height. Not bent, not raised, not spread wide, not crossed.
- SHOULDERS: a natural, smooth, sloped shoulder line, symmetrical left and right. No boxy or inflated shoulders.
- BODY: filled with natural volume as on a real torso, straight hem, the zip or button placket straight down the exact middle. Not flat like a lay-flat photo and not puffed up like a balloon.
- COLLAR: standing naturally, with the inside back of the collar and the inside label visible.
- FABRIC: only the small, realistic folds, quilting and seams of the real product. Smooth, clean, ironed look. No random wrinkles.
- CAMERA AND LIGHT: straight-on, camera at chest height, no perspective distortion, soft even studio light with a very soft subtle shadow under the garment. Sharp, high detail.
- I will send a front photo and a back photo of the product. Make a FRONT image from the front photo and a BACK image from the back photo. Keep the same size, shape and light in both.
```

## 3. Prompts (attach the photos each time)

### PROMPT B: model full-body (attach product photo + man's photo)
```
Product photo and the man's photo attached. Put this exact product on this exact man, following the project rules. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo or detail. One image only.
POSE: [paste one pose line]
```

Pose lines (use a different one for each product):
- Standing straight, front view, arms relaxed.
- Right hand in the trouser pocket, body slightly turned, looking at the camera.
- Left hand in the pocket, body turned about 30 degrees to the side, looking at the camera.
- Three-quarter angle from the corner, weight on one leg, hands relaxed, looking slightly off camera.
- Walking one step toward the camera, arms natural.
- Both hands in the pockets, shoulders relaxed, looking at the camera.
- Close crop from waist up, slight turn, one hand on the zip or collar.

Tip: hands in pockets hide part of a jacket. For products with side pockets or zips, use a pose where the hands are free.

### PROMPT D: model close-up (attach product photo + man's photo again)
```
Product photo and the man's photo attached. Make a CLOSE-UP of this exact man wearing this exact product, following the project rules. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo or detail. One image only.
FRAMING: [paste one framing line]
```

Framing lines:
- Jackets and coats: Chest-up crop, slight turn, collar and zip clearly visible, the chest logo sharp.
- Sweatshirts, sweaters, polos: Chest-up crop, the chest logo and the collar sharp, fabric texture visible.
- Zip-necks: Chest-up crop, one hand lightly on the zip, zip pull and collar sharp.
- Pants: Waist-to-thigh crop, the logo, the pocket and the drawstring sharp, one hand in the pocket.
- Gilets: Chest-up crop, the stand collar, the zip and the chest badge sharp.
- If the face drifts: Neck-down crop, face not shown.

### PROMPT C1: ghost mannequin FRONT (attach the front photo)
```
Product front photo attached. Professional ghost mannequin FRONT view (invisible mannequin, like an official brand website photo), following the project rules. The garment is worn by an invisible body, full and natural, perfectly straight-on and symmetrical. Both sleeves hang straight down beside the body, slightly away from it, parallel and the same on both sides, with the cuffs at the same height. Natural sloped shoulders, the inside of the collar visible. NO visible mannequin, neck block, stand, hanger, head or hands. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC, wide shot with a clear empty grey margin around the garment (at least 12% on every side), centered, never close to the edges. Do not change any logo or detail. One image only.
```

### PROMPT C2: ghost mannequin BACK (same chat, attach the back photo)
```
Back photo attached. Professional ghost mannequin BACK view of the same garment, following the project rules. Same size, shape, framing (wide shot, clear grey margin on every side, centered), sleeve position (hanging straight down beside the body, symmetrical) and light as the front image. Straight-on and symmetrical, the inside of the collar visible. NO visible mannequin of any kind. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Show the back exactly as in the back photo. Do not change any logo or detail. One image only.
```
If a plastic mannequin still appears, send this one line: "Remove the mannequin completely. Ghost mannequin only: the garment alone, floating."

### Style reference (optional)
Optional, for the best result: attach ONE example photo of a professional ghost mannequin (from a brand website) and add this line to Prompt C1 or C2:
"Match the style of the attached style reference only: the sleeve position, the symmetry, the shape and the light. Take the product, its color and every detail ONLY from the product photo."
Use the example only as a private reference. Never upload it to the shop.

If the sleeves still come out angled, send: "Redo it: both sleeves hang straight down beside the body, parallel and symmetrical."

## 4. Order of photos in Shopify
1. Your own real photo
2. Model full-body
3. Model close-up
4. Ghost mannequin front
5. Ghost mannequin back

Photo size in Shopify: 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC.

## 5. Check EVERY image before uploading
- [ ] The logo: letters, position and size match the real product
- [ ] The real color (not brighter or darker)
- [ ] Buttons, zips and zip pulls
- [ ] Collar, cuffs, hem and pockets
- [ ] The man's face looks like the real man
- [ ] Hands and fingers look natural
- [ ] No extra text, watermark or logo added
- [ ] Ghost mannequin: no visible mannequin, neck block, stand or hanger
- [ ] Background is plain #DCDCDC and the size is square
- [ ] The garment is about 75% of the picture width, with a clear grey margin on every side, centered

If one detail is wrong and keeps coming back, don't use that image. Write one short line about what is wrong, for example: "the chest logo is wrong, copy it from the photo". Don't regenerate blindly, because every try uses your limit.

## 6. If a photo already made has the garment too big in the picture
Don't regenerate it (it uses your limit). Because the background is flat #DCDCDC, make the garment smaller yourself:
1. Make a new square canvas, 1200x1200, filled with color #DCDCDC.
2. Put the picture on it, scale it down to about 80% of its size (so the garment ends up about 75% of the width), and center it.
3. Export at 1200x1200. Canva, Photoshop, or any photo editor can do this.
4. Use the SAME scale for the front and the back of the same product so they match.
If the edges of the original picture show (a slightly different grey), set the original's edge to blend, or ask ChatGPT once: "Zoom out, add more empty #DCDCDC space around the garment, same garment."

If ChatGPT keeps making the garment too big, add this one line to your message: "Zoom out. The garment must be about 3/4 of the picture width with empty grey space on all four sides."
