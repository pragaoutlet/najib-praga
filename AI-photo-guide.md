# Praga Outlet: AI photo guide (ChatGPT Plus)

## 0. Setup (once)
1. In ChatGPT make a **Project** (name it "Praga photos").
2. Paste **Section A** into the Project's Instructions box.
3. Use the **normal, fast image mode** (not "thinking"). Only switch to thinking if one product keeps coming out wrong.
4. The man in the photos must have agreed (in writing) to have his photos used in the shop's ads and website.

## 1. For each product (follow in order)
1. Open a **new chat inside the Project**.
2. **Image 1, model full-body:** attach the product photo + the man's photo, send **Prompt B** with one pose line.
3. **Image 2, model close-up:** same chat. Attach the **product photo + the man's photo again**, send **Prompt D** with one framing line. Do NOT attach the first AI picture.
4. **Images 3 and 4, mannequin front and back:** attach the front photo + the back photo of the product, send **Prompt C**.
5. Check every image (Section 5), then upload to Shopify.
6. Next product: new chat.

Per product = 4 AI images. Limit is about 40-50 images per 3 hours (unofficial, check the banner in the app), so about 10 products per 3 hours. Work in batches. Make 1 image per request, and only regenerate when something is wrong.

Exception: if the close-up looks like a different man or different outfit, add the first AI picture and write: "Use the first attached picture only for the man's face and outfit. Copy the product from the product photo." Then check the logo carefully.

## 2. SECTION A: Project instructions (paste once)

```
You create e-commerce photos for Praga Outlet, a men's branded clothing store in Lebanon.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- Use the uploaded product photo(s) as the only truth. Copy the product exactly: color, fabric look, logo, patches, buttons, zips, pockets, stripes, collar, cuffs, hem and fit.
- Never add, remove, move or redraw any logo, text or detail. If unsure about a detail, keep it as in the photo.
- Keep the real color of the product. Do not make it brighter or change its shade.
- Size: square, 1200x1200 pixels.
- Background: plain flat light grey, color #DCDCDC, no texture, no props, no text.
- Soft, even studio light. Sharp, natural, realistic photo, like a real camera photo. Natural skin, no smoothing, no plastic or CGI look.
- No watermark, no captions, no extra people, no brand names added.
- Make only ONE image per request, unless I ask for more.

MODEL PHOTOS
- Use the EXACT man in the uploaded person photo. Keep his real face, hair, beard, skin and body. Do not change or beautify him.
- Plain dark trousers and plain shoes unless the product is trousers. Nothing with logos.
- The whole product must stay clearly visible. Natural, relaxed, confident poses, not stiff.
- I will tell you the pose for each image.

MANNEQUIN PHOTOS
- A plain light grey headless mannequin, the whole product visible, nothing else.
- I will send a front photo and a back photo of the product. Make a FRONT image from the front photo and a BACK image from the back photo. Keep the same mannequin, size and light in both.
```

## 3. Prompts (attach the photos each time)

### PROMPT B: model full-body (attach product photo + man's photo)
```
Product photo and the man's photo attached. Put this exact product on this exact man, following the project rules. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo or detail. One image only.
POSE: [paste one pose line]
```

Pose lines (use a different one for each product):
- Standing straight, front view, arms relaxed.
- Right hand in the trouser pocket, body slightly turned, looking at the camera.
- Left hand in the pocket, body turned about 30 degrees to the side, looking at the camera.
- Three-quarter angle from the corner, weight on one leg, hands relaxed, looking slightly off camera.
- Walking one step toward the camera, arms natural.
- Both hands in the pockets, shoulders relaxed, looking at the camera.
- Close crop from waist up, slight turn, one hand on the zip or collar.

Tip: hands in pockets hide part of a jacket. For products with side pockets or zips, use a pose where the hands are free.

### PROMPT D: model close-up (attach product photo + man's photo again)
```
Product photo and the man's photo attached. Make a CLOSE-UP of this exact man wearing this exact product, following the project rules. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo or detail. One image only.
FRAMING: [paste one framing line]
```

Framing lines:
- Jackets and coats: Chest-up crop, slight turn, collar and zip clearly visible, the chest logo sharp.
- Sweatshirts, sweaters, polos: Chest-up crop, the chest logo and the collar sharp, fabric texture visible.
- Zip-necks: Chest-up crop, one hand lightly on the zip, zip pull and collar sharp.
- Pants: Waist-to-thigh crop, the logo, the pocket and the drawstring sharp, one hand in the pocket.
- Gilets: Chest-up crop, the stand collar, the zip and the chest badge sharp.
- If the face drifts: Neck-down crop, face not shown.

### PROMPT C: mannequin front + back (send the front photo and the back photo together)
```
Front photo and back photo of the product attached. Make 2 images, following the project rules: 1) FRONT view on the plain headless mannequin, 2) BACK view on the same mannequin. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo or detail. Show the back exactly as in the back photo.
```
(This one allows 2 images, because front and back are two photos.)

## 4. Order of photos in Shopify
1. Your own real photo
2. Model full-body
3. Model close-up
4. Mannequin front
5. Mannequin back

Photo size in Shopify: 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC.

## 5. Check EVERY image before uploading
- [ ] The logo: letters, position and size match the real product
- [ ] The real color (not brighter or darker)
- [ ] Buttons, zips and zip pulls
- [ ] Collar, cuffs, hem and pockets
- [ ] The man's face looks like the real man
- [ ] Hands and fingers look natural
- [ ] No extra text, watermark or logo added
- [ ] Background is plain #DCDCDC and the size is square

If one detail is wrong and keeps coming back, don't use that image. Write one short line about what is wrong, for example: "the chest logo is wrong, copy it from the photo". Don't regenerate blindly, because every try uses your limit.
