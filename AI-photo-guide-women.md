# Praga Outlet: AI photo guide for WOMEN (ChatGPT Plus)

Same system as the men's guide, adapted for women's pieces (sweaters, cardigans, dresses, skirts, pants, jeans, jackets, sets).

## 0. Setup (once)
1. In ChatGPT make a **new Project** (name it "Praga women photos"). Keep it separate from the men's Project.
2. Paste **Section A** into the Project's Instructions box.
3. Use a **normal chat inside the Project** (NOT "Work" / agent mode) on the **Instant** model. Do not use "thinking" unless one product keeps coming out wrong.
4. The woman in the photos must have agreed (in writing) to have her photos used in the shop's ads and website.

## 1. For each product (follow in order)
1. Open a **new chat inside the Project**.
2. **Image 1, model full-body:** attach the product photo + the woman's photo, send **Prompt B** with one pose line.
3. **Image 2, model close-up:** same chat. Attach the **product photo + the woman's photo again**, send **Prompt D** with one framing line. Do NOT attach the first AI picture.
4. **Images 3 and 4, ghost mannequin front, then back:** send **Prompt C1** with the front photo, then **Prompt C2** with the back photo (one image each).
5. Check every image (Section 5), then upload to Shopify.
6. Next product: new chat.

Per product = 4 AI images. Limit is about 40-50 images per 3 hours (check the banner), so about 10 products per 3 hours. Work in batches, 1 image per request, regenerate only when something is wrong.

Exception: if the close-up looks like a different woman or a different outfit, add the first AI picture and write: "Use the first attached picture only for the woman's face and outfit. Copy the product from the product photo."

**Sets (top + bottom sold together):** attach the photos of BOTH pieces in every request and say "Show the complete set: top and bottom together, same fabric and color." For the ghost mannequin of a set, make one front image and one back image with both pieces together.

**Several colors of the same product:** do the full 4 images for the first color. For the other colors, attach the new color's photos and add: "Same pose, framing and light as before, only this color."

## 2. SECTION A: Project instructions (paste once)

```
You create e-commerce photos for Praga Outlet, a women's branded clothing store in Lebanon.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- Use the uploaded product photo(s) as the only truth. Copy the product exactly: color, fabric look, pattern, stripes, logo, buttons, zips, pockets, ruffles, pleats, seams, neckline, collar, cuffs, hem, length and fit.
- Never add, remove, move or redraw any logo, text or detail. If unsure about a detail, keep it as in the photo.
- Keep the real color of the product. Do not make it brighter or change its shade.
- Size: square, 1200x1200 pixels.
- FRAMING: wide shot, camera pulled back. The garment must be SMALLER in the picture, with a clear empty grey margin all around it: at least 12% of the picture width of empty space on the left and right, and at least 12% of the picture height on the top and bottom. The garment takes about three quarters of the picture width. It must never touch or come close to the edges. Centered.
- Background: plain flat light grey, color #DCDCDC, no texture, no props, no text.
- Soft, even studio light. Sharp, natural, realistic photo, like a real camera photo. Natural skin, no smoothing, no plastic or CGI look.
- No watermark, no captions, no extra people, no brand names added.
- COLLAR TAG: only when I say "no brand tag", show the neckline/collar with NO label, tag or brand name visible. Remove any brand tag from the photo. Do not invent a replacement label.
- Make only ONE image per request, unless I ask for more.

MODEL PHOTOS
- Use the EXACT woman in the uploaded person photo. Keep her real face, hair, skin and body. Do not change, slim or beautify her.
- Styling: simple and neutral. Plain shoes (plain heels, boots or sneakers that suit the outfit). No bags, no big jewelry, no sunglasses, nothing with logos. Keep hair simple and away from the product details.
- Under a top: plain neutral bottom (plain dark trousers or a plain skirt). Under a bottom: plain neutral top. Under an open cardigan or jacket: a plain simple top in a neutral color that does not compete with the product.
- Modest, elegant and natural. Relaxed, confident poses, not stiff and not exaggerated.
- The whole product must stay clearly visible: its length, shape and fit.
- I will tell you the pose for each image.

GHOST MANNEQUIN PHOTOS (professional invisible mannequin, like an official brand website photo)
- The garment looks worn by an invisible body, floating, perfectly front-facing and symmetrical.
- NO visible mannequin of any kind: no plastic or fabric mannequin, no neck block, no stand, no pole, no base, no hanger, no hands, no head, no body, no legs. Nothing but the garment.
- TOPS, SWEATERS, CARDIGANS, JACKETS: sleeves hang straight down along the sides, slightly away from the body (about 10 to 15 degrees), parallel and symmetrical, cuffs at the same height. Natural, smooth, sloped shoulders. Body filled with natural volume, straight hem, buttons/zip straight down the exact middle. Not flat and not puffed up.
- DRESSES: shown as if worn by an invisible body: natural bust and waist shape, skirt falling naturally and symmetrically, straps/sleeves symmetrical.
- SKIRTS AND PANTS: shown as if worn by an invisible body: waistband round and filled, hips natural, legs falling straight and symmetrical, hem even. No visible mannequin legs.
- NECKLINE/COLLAR: shaped naturally, with the inside back of the neckline and the inside label area visible.
- FABRIC: only the small, realistic folds, knit texture, pleats and seams of the real product. Smooth, clean, ironed look. No random wrinkles.
- CAMERA AND LIGHT: straight-on, camera at chest height (waist height for skirts and pants), no perspective distortion, soft even studio light with a very soft subtle shadow under the garment. Sharp, high detail.
- I will send a front photo and a back photo. Make a FRONT image from the front photo and a BACK image from the back photo. Keep the same size, shape and light in both.
```

## 3. Prompts (attach the photos each time)

### PROMPT B: model full-body (attach product photo + woman's photo)
```
Product photo and the woman's photo attached. Put this exact product on this exact woman, following the project rules. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo, pattern or detail. Show the full length of the product. One image only.
POSE: [paste one pose line]
```

Pose lines (use a different one for each product):
- Standing straight, front view, arms relaxed.
- One hand lightly on the hip, body slightly turned, looking at the camera.
- Three-quarter angle, weight on one leg, hands relaxed, looking slightly off camera.
- Walking one step toward the camera, arms natural, a slight natural movement.
- Hands loosely in front, shoulders relaxed, soft look at the camera.
- Side profile turned slightly back toward the camera, to show the shape and the length.
- Waist-up crop, slight turn, one hand lightly touching the collar or sleeve.

Tips:
- **Dresses and skirts:** use a pose with the legs visible and the arms free, so the full length and the way it falls are clear.
- **Open cardigans and jackets:** use a pose with the arms away from the front so the closure and the front are visible.
- **Pants and jeans:** full-body, standing straight or one step walking, so the full leg line and hem are visible.

### PROMPT D: model close-up (attach product photo + woman's photo again)
```
Product photo and the woman's photo attached. Make a CLOSE-UP of this exact woman wearing this exact product, following the project rules. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Do not change any logo, pattern or detail. One image only.
FRAMING: [paste one framing line]
```

Framing lines:
- Sweaters and knits: Chest-up crop, slight turn, the neckline and the knit texture sharp, any heart, stripe or embroidery clear.
- Cardigans: Chest-up crop, the buttons or zip, the neckline and the knit texture sharp.
- Jackets and coats: Chest-up crop, slight turn, collar, zip or buttons clearly visible.
- Dresses: Waist-up crop, the neckline, straps/sleeves and the fabric detail sharp.
- Skirts: Waist-to-knee crop, the waistband, the pleats or fabric drape sharp.
- Pants and jeans: Waist-to-thigh crop, the waistband, button, belt loops, pockets and stitching sharp.
- Sets: Waist-up crop showing the top, with the waistband of the bottom visible.
- If the face drifts: Neck-down crop, face not shown.

### PROMPT C1: ghost mannequin FRONT (attach the front photo; for a set, attach both pieces)
```
Product front photo attached. Professional ghost mannequin FRONT view (invisible mannequin, like an official brand website photo), following the project rules. The garment is worn by an invisible body, full and natural, perfectly straight-on and symmetrical. [For tops/jackets: both sleeves hang straight down beside the body, slightly away from it, parallel and the same on both sides.] [For skirts/pants: waistband filled, legs falling straight and symmetrical.] NO visible mannequin, neck block, stand, hanger, head, hands or legs. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC, wide shot with a clear empty grey margin around the garment (at least 12% on every side), centered, never close to the edges. Do not change any logo, pattern or detail. One image only.
```

### PROMPT C2: ghost mannequin BACK (same chat, attach the back photo)
```
Back photo attached. Professional ghost mannequin BACK view of the same garment, following the project rules. Same size, shape, framing (wide shot, clear grey margin on every side, centered), sleeve/leg position and light as the front image. Straight-on and symmetrical, the inside of the neckline visible. NO visible mannequin of any kind. 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC. Show the back exactly as in the back photo. Do not change any logo, pattern or detail. One image only.
```
If a plastic mannequin still appears, send this one line: "Remove the mannequin completely. Ghost mannequin only: the garment alone, floating."

If the sleeves or legs still come out angled, send: "Redo it: sleeves/legs hang straight down, parallel and symmetrical."

### Style reference (optional)
Attach ONE example photo of a professional ghost mannequin (from a brand website) and add to Prompt C1 or C2: "Match the style of the attached style reference only: the shape, the symmetry and the light. Take the product, its color and every detail ONLY from the product photo." Use it only as a private reference. Never upload it to the shop.

### One-size products
Many women's pieces are "one size". Do NOT ask the AI to make it look tight or loose by itself. Add one line to Prompt B/D: "The garment fits the woman in a relaxed, natural way, as shown in the product photo." If it comes out too tight or too long, say: "Keep everything the same. Make the fit as in the product photo."

## 3b. Small details the AI gets wrong (buttons, zips, tags, logos, hearts, stripes)
1. **Attach a close-up photo of the detail** as an extra picture, together with the product photo and the woman's photo.
2. **Add a detail-lock line** to Prompt B, D, C1 or C2:
   "DETAIL LOCK: [describe the detail], exactly as in the close-up photo. Copy it exactly, do not simplify it, do not change its color, shape or position."
3. **Always give the SIZE of a small detail**, for example: "The heart is small, about 6 cm wide, on the left chest only."
4. **Stripes and patterns:** say the number and the order of colors: "Stripes are horizontal, alternating dark blue and white, same width, running all around the body and the sleeves."
5. **Buttons and zips:** if they are the same color as the fabric, the AI cannot see them. Say: "Tonal buttons, same color as the fabric, 6 buttons, evenly spaced." Count them.
6. **If it is still wrong, do NOT regenerate the whole picture.** Send one short fix in the same chat: "Keep everything the same. Change ONLY [the detail]: [describe it]."
7. **If a detail comes out too big,** click **Markup** in the ChatGPT image screen, circle the detail and type: "Keep everything the same. Make ONLY this [detail] about half its current size."
8. **If it fails after 2 tries,** use the image anyway and fix the detail by hand (Canva or Photoshop), or use your own real photo for that angle.

Example detail-lock lines:
- Sweater with a heart: "DETAIL LOCK: the red heart is small, about 7 cm wide, on the left chest only. No other hearts. The stripes are horizontal and continue on the sleeves."
- Cardigan: "DETAIL LOCK: 7 buttons, same color as the knit, evenly spaced down the middle. The neckline is a V neck exactly as in the photo."
- Satin skirt: "DETAIL LOCK: the skirt is satin with a soft shine and falls in smooth folds. Keep the exact length shown in the photo and the plain elastic waistband."
- Any garment: "DETAIL LOCK: copy the zip pull, buttons, labels, logos and stitching exactly from the close-up photos. If you are not sure about a detail, leave it as in the photo."

## 4. Order of photos in Shopify
1. Your own real photo
2. Model full-body
3. Model close-up
4. Ghost mannequin front
5. Ghost mannequin back

Photo size in Shopify: 1200x1200, background #DCDCDC.

## 5. Check EVERY image before uploading
- [ ] The pattern, stripes, logo or heart: shape, position and size match the real product
- [ ] The real color (not brighter or darker)
- [ ] Number and color of buttons, zips and zip pulls
- [ ] Neckline, sleeves, cuffs, hem, waistband and length match the real product
- [ ] The woman's face looks like the real woman
- [ ] Hands and fingers look natural
- [ ] No extra text, watermark, bag, jewelry or logo added
- [ ] Ghost mannequin: no visible mannequin, neck block, stand, hanger or legs
- [ ] For sets: both pieces match each other and the real set
- [ ] Background is plain #DCDCDC and the size is square
- [ ] The garment is about 75% of the picture width, with a clear grey margin on every side, centered

If one detail is wrong and keeps coming back, don't use that image. Write one short line about what is wrong and fix only that, using Markup. Don't regenerate blindly, because every try uses your limit.

## 6. If a photo already made has the garment too big in the picture
Don't regenerate it. Because the background is flat #DCDCDC, make the garment smaller yourself:
1. Make a new square canvas, 1200x1200, filled with color #DCDCDC.
2. Put the picture on it, scale it down to about 80%, and center it.
3. Export at 1200x1200 (Canva, Photoshop, or any photo editor).
4. Use the SAME scale for the front and the back of the same product.

Or add this line to your message: "Zoom out. The garment must be about 3/4 of the picture width with empty grey space on all four sides."
